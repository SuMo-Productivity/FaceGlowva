Privacy Policy — Face Glowva

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 21 September 2026

In short: Face Glowva is built to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data, and the app can be used without an account or an internet connection.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Face Glowva mobile application ("Face Glowva", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Face Glowva is published by Sumo Solutions. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do NOT collect

We do not require an account and do not collect personal information such as your name, email, contacts, or location on our servers. Any details you enter (for example, a first name or your goals during onboarding) are stored only on your device and are used to personalize your plan.

2. Camera (optional "mirror")

During a workout you may turn on an optional front-camera "mirror" so you can watch yourself follow along. This is a live preview only — the camera image is never recorded, saved, or uploaded, and it stops when you turn the mirror off or leave the workout. Camera access is requested only when you enable this feature.

3. Photos (progress diary)

If you add before/after photos, they are chosen via your device's system photo picker and stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else, and you can delete them at any time from within the app.

4. Notifications

If you set a daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

5. Where your data lives

Your progress, streaks, settings, plan selections, and any photos are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We operate no server that stores your personal data.

6. Data sharing & selling

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

7. AI-generated imagery

Some images in the app are AI-generated illustrations of non-real people, used for design and demonstration. They are not photographs of real users and are not your data.

8. Children

Face Glowva is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Permissions summary

  • Camera — optional workout mirror (live preview only; not stored).
  • Notifications — optional daily reminders (local only).
  • Photos — chosen through the system picker for your on-device progress diary.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page.
